_Smart Multilingual Land Records Digitization & Verification Platform_
_Smart India Hackathon 2026 | Problem Statement ID 26018_
_Ministry of Rural Development (Department of Land Resources)_

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🌟 *1. WHAT IS THIS IN 20 SECONDS?*
Imagine a government office packed with millions of 50-year-old crumbling paper registers containing land ownership records written in Hindi, Marathi, etc. 

*BhumiDrishti* is an AI-powered web platform that:
📸 Takes photos/scans of old paper registers.
🔍 Uses AI vision (OCR) to read regional Indian languages.
🧠 Extracts owner names, survey numbers, and plot areas.
🛡️ Checks for fraud, title tampering, and duplicate records.
🗺️ Plots the land on an interactive satellite GIS map.
👨‍💼 Gives government officers a split-screen dashboard to verify and approve records in seconds!

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🇮🇳 *2. THE BIG PROBLEM IN INDIA*
Across India, over *300 MILLION* land records are still trapped in physical paper books:
❌ *Decaying Paper:* Moisture, dust, and insects are destroying historical registers.
❌ *Language Barrier:* Written in regional scripts (Hindi, Marathi, Telugu) with complex legal shorthand.
❌ *Massive Fraud:* Over *66% of all civil court cases in India* are land disputes! Fraudsters exploit paper records to sell the same plot twice.
❌ *Slow Typing:* Having clerks manually re-type everything takes decades and leads to countless clerical typos.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

⚙️ *3. HOW IT WORKS (STEP-BY-STEP)*

1️⃣ *UPLOAD:*
A village operator uploads scans or PDFs (single or bulk batches up to 20 files).

2️⃣ *MULTI-PASS OCR READING:*
If an old page has faded ink or coffee stains, the system retries reading it using different optical scanning modes (PSM 3 → 6 → 4 → 1) and automatically keeps the clearest result.

3️⃣ *SMART NLP FIELD EXTRACTION:*
It scans the text and understands legal terms:
• Owner name (काश्तकार / खातेदाराचे नाव)
• Survey / Khasra number (सर्वे नं / खसरा)
• Land area (Acres, Hectares, or Bigha)
• Village, Tehsil, and District

4️⃣ *CONFIDENCE SCORE:*
The system calculates how sure it is (0% to 100%). Clear documents are fast-tracked; blurry ones are flagged for officer review.

5️⃣ *ANTI-FRAUD DUPLICATE GUARD:*
It checks if the exact plot already exists, and uses fuzzy algorithms to catch people trying to steal land with slightly altered name spellings.

6️⃣ *HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP WORKSPACE:*
A Revenue Officer (Patwari) sees the original scan on the *left side* and editable extracted fields on the *right side* with color-coded confidence badges (🟢 Green = High, 🟡 Amber = Medium, 🔴 Red = Review).

7️⃣ *GIS MAPPING & CITIZEN PORTAL:*
Approved plots turn green on a live satellite map, and citizens can check their property online without visiting a government office!

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

👥 *4. WHO USES IT? (5 ROLES)*

👨‍💻 *DEO (Data Entry Operator):* Scans and uploads documents.
👨‍💼 *Verifier (Patwari / Inspector):* Verifies questionable scans side-by-side and approves/rejects them.
🏛️ *District Officer (Collector/SDM):* Manages the district, reviews disputes, and publishes records to the public.
📊 *State Admin:* Monitors state-wide digitization progress on analytics heatmaps.
🔐 *Super Admin:* National ministry director managing system settings and security audit logs.
👨‍🌾 *Everyday Citizen:* Can search their land status publicly (private details like Aadhaar are automatically masked for privacy).

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

💻 *5. WHAT DOES THE WEBSITE HAVE? (10 SCREENS)*

1. *Login Page:* Official portal with 1-click demo role logins.
2. *DEO Dashboard:* Single upload drag-and-drop zone.
3. *Bulk Upload Wizard:* Batch upload up to 20 files with live progress bars.
4. *Records Directory:* Filterable list of all digitized land records with CSV/JSON download.
5. *Record Detail:* Full property ledger showing owners, mutation history, and audit timeline.
6. *Verification Workspace:* Split-screen workstation with document zoom/rotate and color-coded confidence pills.
7. *GIS Cadastral Map:* Interactive satellite map showing color-coded plot boundaries.
8. *Analytics Dashboard:* Real-time charts showing processing volume, accuracy rates, and error trends.
9. *Citizen Lookup:* Public search page by Survey Number.
10. *Admin Console:* Security audit trails and AI sensitivity sliders.

🧠 *6. SMART TECH HIGHLIGHTS*

• *Dual-Language Mastery:* Reads Hindi, Marathi, and English revenue documents seamlessly.
• *AI Learning Loop:* Whenever an officer fixes a typo, the system remembers the correction to improve future accuracy.
• *Tamper-Proof Audit Trail:* Every single upload, edit, or approval is permanently logged with timestamps, user details, and IP address.
• *Real Engineering:* Built with the modern MERN stack (MongoDB, Express, React, Node.js) and 100% automated test coverage.
